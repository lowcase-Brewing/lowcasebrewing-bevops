The idea behind BevOps

Make good beer.
Keep learning.
Enjoy the company.

BevOps brings ideas from DevOps—the practice of making systems easier to understand and improve—to the brew day. The goal is to understand our beer well enough to repeat the good bits and improve the rest.

Read the full philosophy →

  1. Document what matters.

    Capture the recipe, the process, and the observations you'll wish you remembered next time.

  2. Experiment intentionally.

    Know what you're trying to learn. Give yourself room to be surprised.

  3. Learn from every batch.

    Repeat successes. Be honest about the misses. Carry the useful lessons forward.

  4. Keep it enjoyable.

    A notebook and a hydrometer belong here just as much as a fermentation dashboard. Good beer with good people is the point.
