# Infrastructure Roadmap

## Overview

One of the goals of BevOps is to improve observability and operational awareness throughout the brewing process.

While brewing can be performed successfully with nothing more than a notebook and careful observation, modern tools provide opportunities to collect, visualize, and analyze brewing data in ways that were previously difficult or impossible.

This document serves as the long-term roadmap for infrastructure and tooling within the BevOps framework.

The objective is not to prescribe a specific technology stack.

The objective is to identify areas where technology can improve repeatability, observability, and continuous improvement.

## Guiding Principles

When evaluating infrastructure solutions, BevOps encourages brewers to prioritize:

* Simplicity
* Reliability
* Repeatability
* Observability
* Maintainability

Technology should support brewing.

Technology should never become the objective.
